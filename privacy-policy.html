Privacy Policy

Screen Time Overlay - Last updated: November 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Screen Time Overlay collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Usage Statistics

Our app collects screen time data to provide you with insights about your device usage. This includes:

  • Time spent on different applications
  • Session duration and frequency
  • App launch times and usage patterns
  • Device interaction data (screen on/off events)

1.2 Device Information

We may collect certain device information to improve app functionality:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and installation date
  • Battery optimization settings
  • Usage statistics permissions status
  • Overlay permission status

1.3 Personal Settings

The app stores your personal preferences locally on your device:

  • Custom goals and limits
  • Display preferences and themes
  • Notification settings
  • App filter configurations

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core Functionality

We use collected data to:

  • Display accurate screen time statistics
  • Provide personalized insights and recommendations
  • Send notifications about usage limits and goals
  • Generate reports and analytics

2.2 App Improvement

We may use anonymized data to:

  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Develop new features and functionality
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Optimize battery usage and system resources

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All your personal data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your usage statistics to external servers without your explicit consent.

3.2 Data Encryption

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data:

  • Local data encryption for sensitive information
  • Secure storage of user preferences
  • Protected access to system permissions

3.3 Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as you use the app. You can delete all data by uninstalling the application or using the app's data reset feature.

4. Permissions and Access

4.1 Required Permissions

Our app requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): To track app usage and screen time statistics
  • Overlay Permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): To display the screen time overlay on top of other apps
  • Foreground Service: To continuously monitor screen time in the background
  • Notification Access: To send usage reminders and alerts
  • Wake Lock: To keep tracking active when screen is off
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: To ensure continuous tracking

4.2 Optional Permissions

Some features may request additional permissions:

  • Internet Access: For app updates (if available in future versions)
  • Storage Access: For exporting screen time data and reports
  • Vibration: For notification feedback

5. Data Backup

Our app uses Android's built-in backup system (Auto Backup) to help preserve your app settings when you switch devices or reinstall the app:

  • Android Auto Backup: Your app settings and preferences may be backed up to your Google account as part of Android's standard backup system
  • Local Storage Only: All screen time data and usage statistics are stored locally on your device and are not included in cloud backups
  • Privacy: Sensitive data (app categories, filters, usage stats) is explicitly excluded from cloud backups for your privacy

Note: We do not use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or cloud synchronization services. All data processing happens locally on your device.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Data Control

You have the right to:

  • View all data collected by the app
  • Export your data in standard formats
  • Delete specific data or all data
  • Control which apps are tracked through app filter settings

6.2 Privacy Settings

You can control your privacy through app settings:

  • Configure which apps are tracked using app filters
  • Enable or disable optional accessibility metrics tracking
  • Manage notification preferences
  • Control data export and sharing options

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Sending you a notification through the app

9. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@screentimeoverlay.com
  • GitHub: https://github.com/ShalConnects/screen-time
  • Issues: Report privacy concerns through our GitHub issues page

Note: This privacy policy applies to the Screen Time Overlay Android application and related web services. By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
